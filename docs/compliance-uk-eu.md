# UK / EU compliance checklist for dropshipping

> Not legal advice. Dropshipping is legal in the UK/EU, but you are the
> **seller/trader** — the legal responsibilities sit with you, not your supplier.
> Confirm specifics with a qualified professional before launch.

## 1. Business & tax basics
- **Register as a business** (sole trader or limited company in the UK).
- **VAT:** register for UK VAT when you exceed the threshold (and you can register
  voluntarily earlier). For EU sales, look at **IOSS** for imports ≤ €150 and the
  EU **OSS** scheme for cross-border B2C VAT. Post-Brexit, goods shipped from
  outside the UK can incur **import VAT/duty** — be transparent about who pays.
- **Configure Shopify taxes** correctly for the regions you sell to.

## 2. Mandatory store pages (Shopify supports all of these)
- **Terms & Conditions / Terms of Service**
- **Privacy Policy** (UK GDPR / EU GDPR — what data you collect, why, lawful basis,
  third parties, retention, user rights, contact).
- **Cookie consent banner** (PECR / EU ePrivacy) — load non-essential cookies
  (ad pixels!) only after consent. Use a Shopify consent app.
- **Refund / Returns policy** reflecting the **14-day cooling-off period**
  (UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 / EU Consumer Rights Directive).
- **Shipping policy** with **honest, realistic delivery times.** Long supplier
  lead times must be disclosed up front — hiding them is a common compliance and
  chargeback trap.
- **Contact details / business identity** (trader name + geographic address).

## 3. Consumer rights you must honour
- 14-day right to cancel/return for most goods, with refunds within 14 days of return.
- Goods must be as described, fit for purpose, of satisfactory quality
  (Consumer Rights Act 2015).
- Clear total price incl. taxes and delivery **before** the customer pays.

## 4. Product safety & restricted goods
- Products sold in UK/EU may need **UKCA / CE marking** and compliant labelling
  (toys, electronics, cosmetics, etc.). You're responsible as the seller.
- Avoid restricted/regulated categories unless you're set up for them:
  supplements & anything with health claims, cosmetics, electricals without
  certification, vapes, CBD, branded/counterfeit goods, medical devices.

## 5. Advertising standards (this is where AI copy gets you in trouble)
UK **ASA/CAP Code** + EU **Unfair Commercial Practices Directive**, plus the ad
platforms' own policies. Your Claude prompts already encode these — keep them:
- **No misleading claims.** Everything must be substantiated.
- **No fake urgency/scarcity**, fake countdowns, or fake "X people viewing".
- **No fake reviews or invented endorsements.** (Fake reviews are now explicitly
  banned under the UK Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act.)
- **No prohibited health/medical claims** ("cures", "guaranteed weight loss").
- **Disclose ads** — paid influencer content must be labelled (#ad).
- **Honest pricing & "was/now" claims** — reference prices must be genuine.

## 6. Data & email
- Collect marketing consent properly; provide one-click unsubscribe (PECR / GDPR).
- Have a lawful basis for processing; honour data-access/deletion requests.
- If you use Claude/Higgsfield on customer data, that's processing — keep it
  minimal and disclose third-party processors in your privacy policy.

## 7. AI-specific cautions
- **Review everything AI generates before it goes public.** Generate to draft.
- Don't let Claude invent specs, certifications, reviews, or guarantees.
- Higgsfield visuals must not misrepresent the product (size, contents, results).
  Showing a product doing something it can't is a misleading-advertising risk.
- Keep a human accountable for published claims.

## Pre-launch compliance gate (tick before you spend on ads)
- [ ] Business registered; VAT position understood (UK + IOSS/OSS if EU)
- [ ] T&Cs, Privacy, Cookies, Returns (14-day), Shipping, Contact pages live
- [ ] Cookie consent gating ad pixels
- [ ] Realistic delivery times stated
- [ ] No restricted products / unverifiable claims
- [ ] Ad creative + copy checked against ASA/CAP and platform policy
- [ ] A human has reviewed all AI-generated public copy
