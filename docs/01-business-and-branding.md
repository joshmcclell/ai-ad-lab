# 1. Business Model & Branding

## Confirmed name: **FlowBase CRM**

Chosen from the shortlist because it is short, brandable, has clear meaning
("base" of customer data + "flow" of automation), and the matching domains/handles
are commonly available. Alternatives if `flowbasecrm.com` is taken: `getflowbase.com`,
`flowbase.app`, `useflowbase.com`.

## What the business is

A simplified, automated CRM sold as a monthly service to small businesses,
freelancers, startups, and service providers. You operate one multi-tenant
platform; each paying client gets their own isolated workspace (a "tenant").

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Price | **£99 / month, fixed retainer per client** |
| Billing | PayPal recurring subscription (one plan, reused for every client) |
| Setup fee | £0 |
| Contract | Rolling monthly, cancel anytime |
| Build cost | £0 (free tiers / open-source only) |
| Base running cost | £0 (see `02-tech-stack.md` for free-tier limits & the one optional ~£10/yr domain) |

## Simple branding guidance

- **Logo concept:** wordmark "FlowBase" in a medium-weight sans; a small icon of
  three connected nodes (a flowing pipeline) to the left. Build it free in Canva
  or **Figma** using the `Inter` typeface.
- **Colour palette:**
  - Primary `#2563EB` (blue — trust, software)
  - Accent `#0EA5E9` (sky — "flow")
  - Success `#16A34A`  ·  Warning `#F59E0B`  ·  Danger `#DC2626`
  - Ink `#0F172A` (text)  ·  Surface `#F8FAFC` (background)
- **Typeface:** Inter (free, Google Fonts) for everything. Headings semibold,
  body regular.
- **Tone of voice:** plain, friendly, no jargon. "Your customers, organised."
- **Tagline options:** "Your customers, organised." / "The CRM that runs itself."
- **Brand assets to create once (all free):** logo PNG/SVG, a favicon, one
  email signature, a one-page sales sheet (Canva), and a 6-slide overview deck.

## Positioning vs. HubSpot/Salesforce

Sell on the three things SMBs actually complain about with the big tools:
**price** (flat £99 vs. per-seat tiers that creep up), **simplicity** (no
6-week onboarding), and **done-for-you setup** (you onboard them in a day).
Do not try to match feature breadth — match time-to-value.
